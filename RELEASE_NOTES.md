The first release of **TENET Hub**: firmware updates and screen packages for the Metakey TENET 70 keyboard (Hexcore PCB) and its 256×32 OLED screen.

Download **TenetHub.exe** below and run it. No installer; Windows 10 or 11.

## What it does

- **Firmware:** installs REVIVER (now v25) from the Firmware tab. It's built on your PC from the official Hexcore 3.0.7 firmware and checked before anything is flashed. Going back to the official firmware is one click.
- **Packages:** live content on the keyboard's screen. **Now Playing** comes included: song, artist, time (with hours on long tracks), progress bar and an audio spectrum.
- **Community packages:** add other people's package repos by link (Packages tab → *Community sources…*). Making your own: [docs/community-packages.md](https://github.com/yuniverse-au/tenet-hub/blob/main/docs/community-packages.md).
- **Screens:** choose which screens the screen button goes through, and in what order.
- **PC stats:** a built-in screen with three numbers. Pick what each one measures: CPU, GPU or disk usage or temperature, or memory usage. The label under each number follows your choice (REVIVER v25).
- **Temperature sensors built in:** one administrator prompt sets them up (PC stats tab); no separate program needed.
- **Image screen:** put an image or GIF on the keyboard, with brightness, contrast, zoom, position and rotation.
- **Boot logo:** your own image when the keyboard powers on.
- **Keyboard clock:** kept in sync with your PC.
- **Burn-in care:** the screen turns off when the PC sleeps or you're away, and package screens shift slightly over time.

Official packages update themselves; community packages update when you click Update; firmware never installs without you clicking Install.

If the keyboard ever stops responding after a firmware update: unplug it, hold **Esc** while plugging it back in (it starts in update mode), then install the official firmware from the Firmware tab. Typing keeps working on every REVIVER version.
